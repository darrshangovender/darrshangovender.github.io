About

Darrshan Govender

Darrshan Govender is an industrial AI engineer in Durban, South Africa, and the founder of Agulhas Code. He builds predictive maintenance, knowledge assistants that answer only from controlled documents, and agents that draft work and stop for human approval before anything is acted on. His focus is heavy industry, mining and processing in particular.

The work is published as open-source repositories under the MIT licence. Each one runs offline with no API keys, says what its results were measured on, and lists its limitations. The industrial systems use synthetic data or simulated models and say so, so their results describe the method, not a real plant.

Agulhas Code is the AI engineering studio he runs in Durban. The repositories described on this site are published under his own name, not the studio's.

He is available for contract and fractional work, and works on UTC+2, one hour behind Riyadh.

Selected work

  • asset-health: predictive maintenance for mining assets, scored one failure at a time.
  • plant-copilot: a bilingual procedures assistant that cites the clause and refuses when the documents are silent.
  • maintenance-agent: a supervised maintenance-planning agent that stops at a human approval gate.
  • guardrail: a safety gateway for model inputs and outputs.
  • mcp-gateway: a hardened MCP server framework with tenant isolation and an audit log.
